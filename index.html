One tap. Light the whole sky.

Every sky is a puzzle with one question: which star?

Tap a star and it sends out a wave of light. Every star the wave touches lights up and sends its own. Choose well and one tap sets the whole sky burning. Choose the obvious star and you will usually be wrong.

What is in it

  • One tap, no hurry. No timers, no lives. Touch and hold any star to see where its light would go.
  • Stars that behave differently. Red dwarfs glow all round, suns throw a crescent, blue giants fire a long beam, pulsars fire two, and a red giant needs two waves to wake.
  • 100 skies in five worlds. Asteroids, dust, black holes and, in the last world, two taps. Every sky's best answer is proven, so three stars always means you found it.
  • A new Daily Sky, the same for everyone, with one attempt and the answer afterwards.
  • Star Rush and Endless Sky for a minute or an evening.
  • Real skies behind the game: ten NASA photographs to collect with stardust you earn by playing.

What it costs

Worlds 1 and 2, the Daily Sky, Star Rush and Endless Sky are free, with occasional adverts. FUSE Complete is one purchase, kept for good: it opens Worlds 3 to 5, gives you every sky theme and removes every advert. There is no subscription.

FUSE is made by Gaborandi. Sky photographs: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI, JPL-Caltech, USGS, Space Science Institute and the Solar Dynamics Observatory.
