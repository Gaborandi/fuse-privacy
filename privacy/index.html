Catch Fire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Catch Fire has no account and no server of its own. We, the makers of the game, collect nothing about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress, stars, stardust, collected themes, settings and Daily Sky streak.
  • Whether you own Catch Fire Complete, as last reported by the App Store.

This is kept in the app's own storage on your device and is included in your device's backups. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Adverts in the free game

The free game shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's software in the app may collect and use information such as a device identifier, your approximate location derived from your network address, how you interact with adverts, and diagnostic data. Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Where the law requires it, Catch Fire asks for your consent before any advert is requested, and you can change that choice later under Store, Settings, Advert privacy choices.
  • iOS asks whether Catch Fire may track your activity across other companies' apps. Catch Fire plays exactly the same whichever you choose, and you can change it in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Adverts are not requested until you have finished a few levels.

Catch Fire Complete removes adverts. An owner of Catch Fire Complete is not shown adverts, and the game does not request them.

Purchases

Catch Fire Complete is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game asks the App Store only whether your Apple Account owns Catch Fire Complete.

Sharing

If you choose to share a Daily Sky result, the game hands a short line of text to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

Reminders

The evening reminder is off unless you turn it on. If you do, the notices are created and shown by your own device; nothing about them is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn the reminder off in the game's Store tab, or in the Settings app.

Children

Catch Fire is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
