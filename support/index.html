Star Cascade Support

Write to a.alameldien@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps.

I bought Star Cascade Complete and it is not unlocked

Open Store in the game and tap Restore purchases. The purchase belongs to the Apple Account that made it, so make sure that account is signed in on this device.

An advert reward was not offered or did not arrive

Adverts depend on your connection and on what is available in your region. If none is ready, the game simply does not offer one; try again a little later. A reward is given only when the advert has played far enough.

I want to change my advert privacy choices

In the game: Store, Settings, Advert privacy choices (shown where a choice applies). In iOS: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

I am stuck on a level

Touch and hold a star to see its wave and the next one. After three tries without a star, the game offers to show you the best star for free.

I want to start again

Store, Settings, Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. This cannot be undone. It does not affect Star Cascade Complete.
